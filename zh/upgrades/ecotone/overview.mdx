## 激活时间戳 {#activation-timestamps}

| 网络        | 激活时间戳                                   |
| --------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `mainnet` | `1710374401` (2024-03-14 00:00:01 UTC)  |
| `sepolia` | `1708534800` (2024-02-21 17:00:00 UTC)  |

Ecotone 升级纳入了 L1 的 Dencun 升级，并采用 EIP-4844 blob 实现数据可用性。

## 执行层 {#execution-layer}

* Cancun (执行层) ：
  * [EIP-1153：瞬态存储操作码](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1153)
  * [EIP-4844：分片 Blob 交易](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4844)
    * [Blob 交易已禁用](/zh/specifications/base-protocol/execution/l2-execution-engine#ecotone-disable-blob-transactions)
  * [EIP-4788：EVM 中的信标区块根](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4788)
    * [L1 信标区块根嵌入到 L2 中](/zh/specifications/base-protocol/execution/l2-execution-engine#ecotone-beacon-block-root)
    * [信标根合约自动部署](/zh/specifications/base-protocol/consensus/derivation#ecotone-beacon-block-roots-contract-deployment-eip-4788)
  * [EIP-5656：MCOPY - 内存复制指令](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-5656)
  * [EIP-6780：SELFDESTRUCT 仅在同一交易内生效](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6780)
  * [EIP-7516：BLOBBASEFEE 操作码](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7516)
    * [BLOBBASEFEE 始终将 1 压入栈](/zh/specifications/base-protocol/execution/l2-execution-engine#ecotone-disable-blob-transactions)
* Deneb (共识层) ：*不适用于 L2*
  * [EIP-7044：已签名自愿退出永久有效](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7044)
  * [EIP-7045：提高证明纳入的最大槽位](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7045)
  * [EIP-7514：新增每纪元最大流失上限](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7514)
